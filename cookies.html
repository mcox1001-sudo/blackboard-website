1. About cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owner. Similar technologies (such as local storage and pixels) work in comparable ways and are also covered by this policy.

2. How BlackBoard uses cookies

On blackboarddeals.com we keep cookie use to a minimum. The categories we are likely to use are:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — these are required for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie preferences or keeping a form submission secure). These cookies do not require consent under PECR because they are essential to delivering a service you have requested.
  • Analytics cookies — these help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. We will only set these cookies after you give explicit consent via the cookie banner.
  • Marketing cookies — used to measure the effectiveness of campaigns and tailor advertising. These cookies will only be set after you give explicit consent.

The BlackBoard mobile application is governed separately by the in app permission flow and our Privacy Policy.

3. Managing your cookie preferences

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking the cookie settings link in the site footer. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect how the site works.

4. Updates to this policy

We will update this page when the full cookie policy is published, and again whenever we add, change or remove cookies. The "Effective Date" at the top of the page will reflect the latest version.
