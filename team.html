The Team

Meet the founders.

Four founders, one floor we keep showing up on. BlackBoard is built by people who actually go out, run rooms and have spent years thinking about how venues and customers find each other in the next 90 minutes.

“ We're building BlackBoard because the best night out is never the one you planned three weeks ago. It's the one you decided on in the last forty minutes. We want to make that decision easier for customers and worth more to the venues they walk into. ”

— The Founders
MC
Co-founder · Product

Michael Cox

UK Sales Director, RoboMercato

Michael leads product at BlackBoard. By day he's UK Sales Director at RoboMercato fixing how UK buyers source robotics; by night he's the one stitching together the BlackBoard product, the venue tooling and the consumer app. He cares about the bit most platforms ignore: making the actual handover between a venue and a customer feel like nothing at all.

Co-founder · Venues & Hospitality

Adam Roy Green

Fractional Executive · ROY Hospitality

Adam runs venue strategy at BlackBoard. Twelve plus years across furnishings, interiors and hospitality, with director-level roles in high growth startups and established firms. He's taken stage zero ventures to market, scaled multi-million-pound D2C brands and led high-end residential and hospitality projects across the UK, Europe and the US. He's why BlackBoard is built for the floor, not the slide deck.
