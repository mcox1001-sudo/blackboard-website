BlackBoard's West
London pilot is live.

After six months of building, the BlackBoard pilot has launched across six West London neighbourhoods. Real venues, real deals, real nights out. Here's what's running and what we're measuring before we scale.

Updates · 2 May 2026 · 4 min read · Michael Cox

As of this week, BlackBoard is live in the wild. Six neighbourhoods. Dozens of venues. Real deals refreshing in real time. If you're inside the pilot zone with the app open, you should be seeing offers within walking distance, right now.

Where the pilot runs

The first phase covers Surbiton, Kingston, Teddington, New Malden, Hampton Wick and Twickenham. We chose this corner of West London on purpose: a dense cluster of independents, strong weekday and weekend trade, and a customer base used to going out close to home.

If you're outside this zone you can still join the waitlist. We'll prioritise the next launch areas based on where demand is loudest.

What's actually on the board

During the pilot, we're focused on four categories of offer that we think are most useful for the spontaneous night out:

  • Food deals — set menus, two-for-one mains, kitchen happy hours.
  • Drink offers — discounted cocktails, wine flights, end-of-night pints.
  • Events & nights out — quiz nights, DJs, live music, comedy.
  • Same-day flash drops — last hour deals from venues clearing kitchens or filling the floor.
"We didn't want a coupon book. We wanted a board that updates the moment a venue has space, surplus or something worth shouting about."

How redemption works during the pilot

Pilot redemption is intentionally simple: staff assisted, no QR codes, no PINs. You confirm the deal with the team at the venue, slide the in app Offer Redeemed toggle, and the screen turns green. The staff member sees it, applies the discount and you're done.

We're watching this flow closely. If it holds up under busy Friday night trade, we'll keep it. If it doesn't, we'll move to a faster, more verifiable mechanism. Either way, the goal is the same: friction free for both sides.

What we're measuring

The pilot has three jobs. We want to know whether we can:

  1. Get high quality, real time deals from venues without burning out their teams.
  2. Send real footfall through doors at the hours venues need filling.
  3. Build the kind of relationship with users where they reach for BlackBoard before Google Maps.

We'll publish what we're seeing in upcoming notes, including the bits that aren't working yet. The point of a pilot is to learn out loud.

Venue in the pilot zone? Get listed in minutes via the new venue sign up flow. There's no contract, no setup fee, and you stay in control of every deal you post.

What's next

Through May and June we're refining the offer mix, onboarding more venues across the pilot zone and tightening the redemption flow. From July, we'll start lining up the next expansion areas based on waitlist density and venue interest.

If you want to be part of phase two, the best thing you can do is join the waitlist and let us know where you'd want BlackBoard live next.
